Kankakee River May 9, 2025 — DEM Bank/Terrain Pilot v1.3.9

USGS 1-meter bare-earth elevation compared with a May 9 water-surface profile reconstructed from USGS gages and Indiana DNR river miles.

Revised pilot metric: Each 0.1-mile cross-section uses the hydro-flattened 1-meter DEM plus river-location and neighboring-cross-section constraints to identify the most plausible river-water surface and its left and right edges. Bank height is reported as the maximum bare-earth terrain elevation above the reconstructed May 9 water surface within 100 feet landward of each detected water edge. River-left/right are defined from the paddler's downstream-facing perspective. The detected channel and 100-ft analysis zones are shown on the cross-section for QA/QC.
