Species Associations / Co-occurrence

Default ecological neighborhood: same survey date + same 0.25-mile river reach. Each species counts once per neighborhood. “Lift” compares observed co-occurrence with the amount expected from each species' overall neighborhood frequency; values >1 indicate greater-than-expected overlap.

River-mile labels are off by default.
GoPro references are assigned using the nearest same-date GoPro GPS record. The displayed source file is the GPS/source filename from the master GoPro reference workbook; its stem identifies the associated recording sequence, and the video time is the approximate within-file timestamp. Spatial match distance is shown for QA. Species co-occurrence is exploratory and does not by itself imply biological interaction. Bird nondetection is not equivalent to species absence; however, PUC environmental telemetry confirms continuous recorder operation throughout each documented kayak survey. Map popups also show the nearest PUC Relative Light Exposure, sound-pressure level, and AQI sensor reading; AQI is retained as an exploratory PUC sensor value rather than an authoritative regulatory air-quality measurement. The draw-a-box tool summarizes only detections currently visible under the active dashboard filters. The river-mile chart can be viewed in 5-mile or 1-mile bins and retains survey date as a separate series; apparent longitudinal patterns may reflect habitat, survey date/season, time of day, or other survey conditions.
